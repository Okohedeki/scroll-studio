## Intent

Launch video for Scroll Studio (github.com/Okohedeki/scroll-studio): a toolkit for scroll-driven marketing
sites, made three ways on one local RTX 4090 with no API keys. Before/after story: a grey Blender blockout
transforms into the finished cinematic AI film, then the other two demos (Mona Lisa drawn live, Cellwright Bio
live 3D), then the pipeline, then the "Scroll Studio" end card. Dark, cinematic, the sites' own aesthetic.
