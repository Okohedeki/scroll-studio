# Style research

Each style is an experience of its own: a scroll mechanic, a layout, transitions and pointer behaviour, not a colour
scheme. Before a style is built, it is researched here: where it comes from, how its layout works, what scrolling and
the pointer do, its components and type, how to implement it, what to avoid, and what it falls back to with reduced
motion. Each brief ends with the "UX signature" that no other style may use.

The [style gallery](../styles.md) says what every style does in one line, and
[how to contribute](../contributing/guide.md#consistency-rules) says what a new style has to include.
