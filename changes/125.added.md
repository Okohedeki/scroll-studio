A brand (`theme.from`) now reaches the parts of a style that are drawn in code, not only its stylesheet: the poster primaries of bauhaus, neon-glow's tubes, the markers of neo-brutalism, the wireframe-3d drawing and screen, pixel-art's palette, zoetrope's printed cards, the plates of smears, the sheet of blueprint and more (about twenty modules and four styles' templates). A style's templates are read through the same colour swap as its stylesheet, and the page sets `--skin-<role>` for each palette role the brand re-colours, which the style's runtime reads through `skin()` in `runtime/src/styles/_kit.ts`. Colours that are not the style's palette (film dyes, pixel-console greys, brass) stay as they were. With no brand nothing is set and every page builds and draws exactly as before. The render tests also load five styles wearing a brand.
