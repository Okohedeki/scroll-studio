A site can wear any brand: `theme: { from: <url | logo | brand.yaml> }` reads a brand's colours, fonts, button radius and logo (once, kept in `.build/brand/`) and lays them over the preset and style, with missing colours derived under the presets' contrast rules; `studio brand my-site --from <source>` writes those tokens into site.yaml so they can be edited. On a style the brand replaces the style's own palette and faces while its experience stays, and a style can declare in `requires:` what a brand may not change (its light or dark room, a face its mechanic is built on). Font roles also take files of the project's own, `fonts: { display: { file: fonts/Brand.woff2 } }`, served from the site. Existing specs build exactly as before.
