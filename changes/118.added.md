`studio remake <url>` reads a live landing page and writes a new project that tells it as a scroll site: its hero picture as the opening scene (a photo parallax, or a film with a GPU), its sections as the blocks their shape calls for (`faq`, `quote`, `stats`, `timeline`, `features`, `strip`, `product`, `cta`, `intro`), its copy and calls to action with their links, its pictures in `inputs/` and its brand in `theme.from` as editable tokens. `--style` puts any style on the brand and `--no-gpu` keeps the opening laptop-friendly. The site's `robots.txt` is honoured and nothing is uploaded; the MCP tool `remake_site` does the same in conversation.
