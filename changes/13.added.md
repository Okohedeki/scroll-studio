One way to place and time words in every scene and layout: a step's `at` puts it at a named spot, in a box on the stage
(`{x, y, w, h}`) or in a slot on the theme's 12-column grid (`{grid: "9 / span 4", row: top}`); `show: [from, to]` sets
when it is on screen; `enter` and `exit` pick how it arrives and leaves (`fade`, `rise`, `wipe`, `type-on`, `scale`,
`none`). Older fields (`place:` corners, `at: [from, to]`, `layout` + `side`, split/cards `length`) keep working and
build the same pages. New example: `examples/margin-notes`.
