Image inputs take `live:`: one small thing alive in a still picture, drawn by the page with no GPU needed. Up to four
details per picture (`sweep`, `glint`, `drift`, `rise`, `flow`) in a box or a prompt's region, with `speed`, `strength` and a
theme `color`, on hero, product, strip and gallery images, backdrops and parallax photos; paused off screen and still with
reduced motion. `examples/kitewell` shows it on its hero.
