Frontier providers in the model router: Google Gemini (images, edits, text and image-to-video), Black Forest Labs FLUX.2 (images and edits), Runway (image-to-video) and OpenAI text, each used only when its key is set. A new `video` job kind lets `models: {prefer: provider}` animate film beats and `theme.art` on a provider instead of the local video model. `studio cloud login | status | buy | logout` signs in to an optional cloud account that runs these jobs on credits for builders without keys; local models and your own keys stay free and come first.
