`studio stills` turns one product photo into pack shots, lifestyle scenes and details: several art directions,
candidates across seeds and backends, critics for product fidelity, framing and contrast, and a varied shortlist per
shot on `stills/board.html`. Set it up with `outputs.stills` in `project.yaml`; it runs on a laptop with drawn
backdrops and uses image, edit and text backends where they can run.
