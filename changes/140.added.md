Film beats take named camera moves: `camera: dolly-in` (or `{move: orbit-left, amount: 0.8, at: [0.5, 0.6]}`) for static, dolly-in, dolly-out, crash-zoom, dolly-zoom, orbit-left, orbit-right, crane-up, crane-down, fpv, whip-pan and push-through, each a tested recipe flown through the start image's depth and followed by LTX-2.3 like keyed moves (keyed `camera:` is unchanged); docs/scenes.md says how well each reads. A film section's `finish:` (`finish: true` for the defaults) runs a finishing pass before the encode: a SeedVR2 restore in larger batches, a light temporal denoise and sharpen, an optional deflicker, and frame interpolation with a RIFE or FILM checkpoint where motion is slow; on test clips it took `studio inspect`'s softness from 0 to 67-88 and the verdict from bad to clean. `subject: {image, name}` keeps one person or product the same across a story film: beats without their own image start on the reference edited into their scene by Qwen-Image-Edit. `studio build --plan` prices the new stages and `studio doctor` lists an installed frame interpolation checkpoint.
