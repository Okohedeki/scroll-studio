Built pages get a polish report too: `studio inspect my-site --page` loads `dist/` in headless Chromium at desktop and phone size and, per section, measures how busy the picture behind each headline is and whether the words pass contrast against the real pixels (with and without a scrim), the type (headline against body size, characters per line, headline lines on a phone), pictures blown up past their pixels or cut through a face, and layout (overflow, a hero button below the fold, text over text). Each finding names the section and the fix in the spec's terms (`place:`, `surface:`, `shade:`, a larger `size:`), a contact sheet marks them on the screenshots, and the command exits 1 when anything fails, so a showcase page can be gated on it.
