The visitor's pointer can reveal the page: `pointer: { mode: lens }` on a `parallax` or `film` section shows the picture's colour (or a second picture of the same view, one per photo) through a soft circle under the pointer, and `pointer: { mode: light }` is a torch, a pool of warm light on a darkened picture that rakes across a photo's depth. On phones it follows the finger, sitting above it; with reduced motion and for keyboard users it rests still. Existing specs build exactly as before.
