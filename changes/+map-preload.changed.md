Map scenes preload their flight: while the loading screen is up the camera visits every stop (and the far point of each long move) and waits for its tiles, keeps them cached, and draws labels without fading, so the scroll never waits on the network. Measured on Tidewater Lines in Chrome on the GPU: hitches over 50 ms went from 2-3 per scroll (up to 150 ms) to none, for about 5 s more on the loading screen (capped at 10 s, 6 s on phones). Routes are added once and revealed with a gradient instead of being rebuilt every frame.
