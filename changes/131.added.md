Art-directed pictures in one medium: `imagery.medium` can name `risograph`, `toile`, `engraving`, `gouache`, `watercolour`, `halftone` or `ink`, a tested local recipe that phrases every Z-Image prompt in that medium and then post-processes every generated still on the CPU (two-ink riso separation with grain and misregistration, one-ink toile and engraving on paper, a real dot screen, flattened matte gouache, washes that pool on paper, brush ink with washes), so the whole page reads as one hand. The inks come from the theme and the brand (`theme.from`): a blue toile for a blue brand, a risograph in accent and accent2, overridable with `imagery.inks` and `imagery.paper`. Any image can take its own `medium:` (a file too), and `calm: left | right | center | top | bottom` keeps that side quiet for a headline: asked of the model, measured, then mirrored or regenerated with the next seeds; a hero in a named medium gets it from its `align`, sets its words light or dark from the picture and drops the heavy shade. New example: [Kitewell](examples/kitewell/site.yaml), a risograph fintech hero with a toile closing band. Existing specs build exactly as before.
