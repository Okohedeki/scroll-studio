Commerce kit:
- `studio import <url>` starts a project from a product page, with a product profile every output uses.
- `studio concepts` writes ad angles, hooks and scripts.
- Ads come in one variation per concept, at 1k, 2k or 4k.
- `studio ugc` makes vertical creator-style video ads with captions and a voiceover.
- `languages:` writes copy and voice for each market.
- `studio edit` changes a finished picture with an instruction.
