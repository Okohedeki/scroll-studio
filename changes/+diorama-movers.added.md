Dioramas can have things that move: `movers` travel routes (one driven by the scroll, waiting at stops while their steps are on screen and changing what they carry there; others looping on their own), and `traffic` fills a grid with movers that each take their own way and never collide. `camera.travel: glide` follows the scroll mover from place to place, and `camera.yaw` sets the wide views' azimuth. A `plain` world is an empty floor, the kit gains an automation set (printer, placer, oven, scanner, storage tower, robot arm, carrier deck, tiles), places take `turn` and an empty `sign`, and phones frame a place by its width.
