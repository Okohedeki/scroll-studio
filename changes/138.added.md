A landing page kit, in the theme's tokens and every style: a pill `badge` with a link, a tag or a dot (on a hero or any
scene step), buttons with an `icon` (arrow, play, external) and a `look: ghost` secondary, and on a hero a `rating` line,
an inline email `signup` (a plain form posting to a URL you name or a `mailto:`), a row of `stats` parted by thin rules,
a one-colour `logos` strip and `frame: card`, the hero (or any scene) as a rounded card on a border in the theme's
accents. New `logos` and `signup` blocks, and `stats` blocks take `look: divided`. A hero that uses the kit is set in a
calmer type scale; pages that use none of it are unchanged. See the Larkspur example, which builds without a GPU.
