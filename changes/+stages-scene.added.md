A new scene, `type: stages`: one subject through its states (a drawing becoming the thing, a product being made), a sequence of stills of the same view scrolled through in order. Each state arrives by a `fade`, a `wipe` or a `map` (parts land one by one in the order a greyscale picture gives), optionally with a band of light travelling with the front, and the scroll holds on each state while its step is read. `fit` and `phone` frame the pictures (cover or contain), so a phone can keep the whole subject in view below the words.
