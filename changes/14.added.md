A step in any scene can be any block: `block: stats`, `quote`, `features`, `timeline`, `cta`, `gallery`, `product` or `faq`, written with that block's own fields beside `block:`, placed with `at` and timed with `show` like any other step (kicker, title and body are the default `caption` block). A style that draws a block its own way draws it that way inside a scene too.
